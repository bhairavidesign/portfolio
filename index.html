Tinker Village

Strategy • Systems Thinking • Stress Testing • Product Management
Duration

Role
UI/UX Design Lead

Led design and product strategy for Tinker Village, sitting between the founder's vision and the engineering team building it. I traced the platform's instability to a structural gap: there was no information architecture for the team to build against. I validated that with the founder, then translated his half-formed ideas into clear IA, logic flows, and prioritized specs. Managing a team of junior and senior developers, I set direction, kept the team aligned through design and strategy discussions, and stayed close through implementation, taking the platform from 30 APK releases a month down to 3, and catching 90% of use cases before a single line of code was written.
