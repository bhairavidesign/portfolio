DG Lock

DG Lock is an ambitious project in Saudi Arabia designed to create a sustainable and technologically advanced city. Within it, a digital locker provides residents with a secure and convenient solution for managing and storing their personal digital assets, enhancing both security and accessibility.

AMy Role — What did I do to make the product seamless?

In my role, I played a key part in shaping the user experience by:

  • Identifying Problems: Analyzed user research and existing design constraints to pinpoint issues and areas for improvement.
  • Leading Ideation: Facilitated brainstorming sessions and created initial sketches and wireframes to explore and refine design solutions.

  • Collaboration: Worked constantly with the Design, PO, and Dev teams to ensure a seamless experience in the product.

BProblem Statement — What were the challenges faced?

In DG Lock, residents need a secure and user-friendly digital locker to manage and store their digital assets. Current solutions often fall short in security, usability, and integration. The challenge was to create a digital locker that provides robust security and seamless access within DG Lock's advanced technological ecosystem.

CDesign Brief — What was the aim of this project?

To build a digital government platform that acts as a single entity catering to individuals and businesses inside DG Lock, having minimum physical interaction, maintaining the confidentiality of the data, and being the medium between the citizens and the government.

FLessons - What Could Be Worked Upon?

  • Tight Deadline: The tight deadline constrained the project, limiting research, design iterations, and user testing, which could impact the final solution's quality and thoroughness.
  • Limited Testing: Insufficient time almost prevented thorough user testing and iteration, potentially leading to unresolved usability issues.

HMarket Research — What about the competitor apps out there?

Yes, competitors were thoroughly analyzed on a global scale. Features and drawbacks were compared to get a better understanding of where DG Lock could differentiate.

G2User Research — How did the primary research begin?

Initially, the approach was a little confusing. Using the snowballing technique, a survey (via Google Forms) was circulated among friends and colleagues to get a variety of information. To reduce language barriers, only English speakers were surveyed.

Sample size: 54 participants

G3User Research — How were user interviews?

Out of 54 participants, 32 people were ready for the interview. It was an interesting experience to interview people and understand their perspective on the subject. The interviews lasted approximately 45 minutes each. The following questions were asked to each participant:

🙋 Personal Experience

  • Can you describe how you currently manage your digital assets (e.g., documents, photos, passwords)?
  • What challenges do you face with your current digital storage solutions?

🛡️ Security Concerns

  • How important is security when it comes to storing your digital assets?
  • Have you ever experienced a breach of digital security? How did it impact you?

♿ Convenience and Accessibility

  • How often do you access your digital assets, and from which devices?
  • What features would make a digital locker more convenient for you?

👍 Trust and Perception

  • What factors would influence your trust in a digital locker service?
  • How do you feel about a government-backed digital locker solution?

Sample size: 32 participants
